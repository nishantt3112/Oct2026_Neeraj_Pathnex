			- readiness probe

				- this means the application running inside the pod is able to serve the traffic or not this is checked 
				- again at an endpoint which is exposed by the application like /healthz/readyz health-checks are performed and if the app is not able to serve the traffic or there would be high latency found then it will be removed from the service endpoint so that only healthy pods would serve the traffic
